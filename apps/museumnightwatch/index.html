Museum Night Watch — Help

You are the night guard. From 12 AM to 6 AM the exhibits move one room each hour, and dotted lines show where each one goes next. Spend power on light, cameras and doors to keep them out of the office until dawn.

Your tools

  • 💡 Light — 1 power. Lights one room for the hour.
  • 🎥 Camera — 1 power. Watches one room for the hour.
  • 🚪 Door — 2 power. Shuts the west or east office door for the hour.

Each night has a power limit per hour and for the whole night. Tools reset when the hour ends.

The exhibits

  • The Knight — walks one room each hour. Will not step into a lit room.
  • The Wax Gentleman — walks one room each hour, light or not. Freezes while a camera watches his room.
  • The Mummy — rests on the hour, then shuffles two rooms at once on the half. Stops before a lit room.
  • The Raptor Bones — hunts by dark. At a fork it takes the first dark way. Waits if every way is lit.
  • The Clanking Armor — light and cameras do not stop it. Only a shut door sends it back.
  • The Rolling Bust — if the room ahead is lit, it rolls back one room instead.

Anything that walks into a shut door is sent back to where it started.

Questions

How do I play an hour?
Pick Light, Camera or Door at the bottom, tap rooms or doors on the map, then tap End hour.
How do stars work?
Survive until 6 AM for one star. Use one more than the least power possible for two, and exactly the least for three.
Where are the rules for each exhibit?
Open the Journal at the bottom of the screen.
Is it scary?
Spooky, not scary. There are no jump scares, screams or gore.
Can I replay a night?
Yes. Open the list at the top left and pick any night you have reached.
