Museum Night Watch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Museum Night Watch is a puzzle game by an independent developer. It runs entirely on your device, makes no network connections, and gathers no information about who you are or how you play.

Saved on the device

The game keeps two things in its own storage on your device: which nights you have finished with the least power you used on each, and the night you last opened. Nothing else is stored. This is never sent off the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Not in this game

There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and the advertising identifier is not used. The game never asks to use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. The cameras in the game are part of the puzzle and never use your device's camera.

Children

No personal information is collected from anyone, including children.

Updates to this policy

A version that changes any of the above (for example, one that adds ads) will come with an updated policy and a new date here before it is released. Contact: aka84728@gmail.com
