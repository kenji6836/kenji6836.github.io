Museum Night Watch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Museum Night Watch is a puzzle game by an independent developer. It runs entirely on your device, makes no network connections, and gathers no information about who you are or how you play.

Saved on the device

Your logbook stays on your phone. It holds the nights you have survived, your lowest power for each one, and the night you were last on. That is all the game remembers, it never leaves the phone, and deleting the app erases it.

Not in this game

Version 1.0 shows no ads and sells nothing. You do not sign in, and the game does not measure how you play or follow you across other apps. It asks for no permissions at all. The security cameras in the game are drawn on the map; your phone's own camera is never turned on.

Children

Because nothing about the player is gathered, nothing is gathered from children either.

Updates to this policy

If a later update brings in ads or anything else that touches your data, this page will be rewritten and re-dated first. Questions: aka84728@gmail.com
