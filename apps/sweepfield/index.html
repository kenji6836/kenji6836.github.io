Sweepfield help

Sweepfield is a calm minesweeper where opened cells bloom into flowers. Four difficulties, boards picked to be cleared by logic, a 64-flower almanac, and play from start to finish with VoiceOver.

Common questions

  • Will I ever have to guess? Hardly ever. Mines are placed after your first tap, and the game searches for a layout a solver can open by logic alone. Only if none turns up does a board need a guess.
  • Where are my flowers? Tap the bouquet on the home screen to open the Field Almanac. "See bloom rates" shows how often each flower appears.
  • What if I dig up a mine? You can watch a short optional ad to take that one move back (the mine gets a flag), start over, or go home.
  • How do I turn off ads? Buy "Remove Ads" once in Home → Settings. It hides the banner and the full-screen ads. On a new iPhone or iPad, tap "Restore Purchases" in the same place.
