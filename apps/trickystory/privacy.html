Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Oops, Groom! is a story puzzle game: Leo the groom has overslept on his wedding day, and you move things around each scene to find the unexpected answer. You play without an account, and the game has no server of its own. This page covers what the game keeps on your device, what reaches its ad partner, and what you can change.

Kept on your device only

Solved scenesWhich scenes you have solved, so the album fills in and the game reopens where you left off
Play countHow many scenes you have finished or skipped, used to space out full-screen ads
Ad timingWhen the last full-screen ad appeared
SettingsThe Vibration switch
PurchaseWhether you own Remove Ads
Ad choicesIn regions where it applies, your answers on Google's consent form

None of this is sent to the developer.

Motion and touch

Some scenes are solved by shaking or tilting your device. The game reads the motion sensor only while one of those scenes is open, uses it on the spot, and does not save or send it. It never uses the camera, the microphone, your photos, or your contacts.

Ads

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. No ad appears while you are solving a scene. A full-screen ad may appear when you tap "Next scene" on the card after a scene, but never in the first three scenes, at most once every three scenes, and at least three minutes apart. Hints are unlocked by watching a short video ad. If the video cannot load, you get the hint anyway.

When an ad is requested, AdMob may receive your IP address, a rough location, information about your device, which ads were shown and whether you tapped them, and the advertising identifier of your device if you allow tracking. Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent and tracking

Right after the game opens, Google's consent form appears first where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland). Apple's tracking question comes after it, and only if your answers there allow it. Saying no to either changes nothing in the game. To review your ad choices later, open the album, tap the gear, and choose "Ad Privacy Choices." To change tracking, go to iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Remove Ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple; the game never sees your payment details. After buying, full-screen ads stop and hints come right away with no video. Use "Restore Purchases" in the album's settings on a new device or after reinstalling.

Children

The game is not directed mainly at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it kept. Your purchase record stays with Apple and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

これらを開発者に送ることはありません。

本体の動きと指の操作

本体を振る、傾けると解ける場面があります。動きのセンサーはその場面を開いている間だけ読み、その場で使うだけで、保存も送信もしません。カメラ、マイク、写真、連絡先は使いません。

広告

広告はGoogle AdMobから出します。場面を解いている間には出しません。全画面の広告は、場面の後の札で「次の場面へ」を押したときに出ることがあります。はじめの3場面には出さず、3場面に1回までで、3分以上あけます。ヒントは短い動画の広告を見て受け取ります。動画が読み込めないときも、ヒントはそのまま受け取れます。

広告を出すときに、AdMobに次の情報が届くことがあります。IPアドレス、おおまかな位置、端末の情報、どの広告が出て押されたか、追跡を許可したときは端末の広告IDです。Googleの使い方はGoogleの説明で確かめられます。

同意と追跡の許可

ゲームを開いた直後に、法律で必要な地域（EEA、英国、スイスなど）では、まずGoogleの同意フォームを出します。Appleの追跡の許可は、その後に、同意フォームの答えが許すときだけ聞きます。どちらを断っても、ゲームは変わりません。広告の選択を見直すときは、アルバムを開いて歯車を押し、「広告のプライバシーの選択」を選びます。追跡の許可は、iPhoneの「設定」＞「プライバシーとセキュリティ」＞「トラッキング」で変えられます。

「広告を消す」

「広告を消す」は1回払いで、支払いはAppleが受け付けます。ゲームが支払いの情報を見ることはありません。買うと全画面の広告が止まり、ヒントも動画なしですぐ受け取れます。機種を替えたときや入れ直したときは、アルバムの設定の「購入を復元」を押します。

子ども

このゲームは、13歳未満の子どもを主な対象に作ったものではありません。

情報を消すには

アプリを消すと、残していた情報もすべて消えます。買い物の記録はAppleに残るので、復元できます。
