Thimble — Support

Thimble is a climbing puzzle seen from the side. A tiny gnome stands in a room of shelves, and a silver thimble waits somewhere higher up. Pull drawers out and push them back in to turn the furniture into a staircase, then climb to the thimble.

There are 30 shelves in a sewing box, a bookcase and a kitchen cupboard. There are no timers and no luck, and the game plays fine with no signal. There are no ads and no purchases. There is nothing to sign up for, and your progress lives only on that device.

FAQ

  • How do I play? Use the arrows to walk left and right. Face a drawer and tap Pull to slide it out one step, or Push to slide it back in. You can also swipe on the shelf: sideways to walk, down to pull, up to push.
  • The gnome won't climb. The gnome climbs only one step at a time. If the next step is two high, open another drawer below it first.
  • A drawer is in my way. Push it back in, or walk around it. The order in which you open drawers is the puzzle.
  • I made a wrong move. Tap Undo to take back one move, or Retry to start the shelf again. Use them as often as you like.
  • How do stars work? Reach the thimble in the best number of moves, shown under the buttons, to earn three stars.
  • How do I pick a shelf or turn off the sound? The grid button at the top opens the list of shelves. The speaker button next to it turns the sound on and off.

横から見た棚の部屋で、小人が指ぬきまで登るパズルです。引き出しを引いて出し、押して戻して、家具を階段に変えます。

よくある質問

  • どう遊びますか？ 矢印で左右に歩きます。引き出しの方を向いて Pull を押すと1つ手前に出て、Push を押すと奥へ戻ります。棚の上をなぞっても動かせます（横で歩く、下で引く、上で押す）。
  • 小人が登りません。 小人が登れるのは1段ずつです。2段の高さがあるときは、間にもう1つ引き出しを出します。
  • 引き出しが邪魔をします。 押して戻すか、回り道をします。どの順に引き出すかが、この遊びの考えどころです。
  • 手を間違えました。 Undo で1手戻せます。Retry で棚の最初からやり直せます。どちらも何度でも使えます。
  • 星の数は？ ボタンの下に出る一番短い手数で着くと、星3つです。
  • 棚を選ぶには？音を消すには？ 上のます目のボタンで棚の一覧が開きます。隣のスピーカーのボタンで音を切り替えます。
