SortSafe – Water Sort Puzzle — Support

SortSafe is a water sort puzzle designed for color-blind players. Every color also has its own symbol, every level is checked by a solver so it can always be cleared, and every bottle can be read out by VoiceOver.

FAQ

  • Is every level really solvable? Yes. All levels are generated and then solved by a computer solver before they ship. The "Par" shown on each level is the solver's move count.
  • I can't tell the colors apart. Each color has a fixed symbol (circle, triangle, star, and so on). Turn on Settings → "Bold symbols" to make them stronger. It turns on automatically when iOS "Differentiate Without Color" is on.
  • What does "No way forward" mean? The solver found that the current board can no longer be cleared. Use Undo or Restart; you can also add one extra bottle per level.
  • Does it work with VoiceOver? Yes. Each bottle is read from top to bottom, for example "Bottle 3: 2 Red circle, 1 Blue hexagon, 1 space left".
  • How do I remove ads? Settings → Remove Ads (one-time purchase). Use "Restore Purchases" on a new device.
