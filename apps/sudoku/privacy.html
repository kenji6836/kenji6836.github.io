Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Sudoku: Classic Puzzle & Hints ("the App") is a number puzzle with step-by-step solving hints. This page explains what happens to information while you solve puzzles in it, and which outside services are involved.

Information We Collect

Solving puzzles needs no personal information, and the App does not ask for any. Your puzzle data (the game in progress, notes, statistics, Daily Challenge results and streak, hint coin balance, and settings such as sound, haptics, and theme) is stored only on your device using the system's local storage (UserDefaults) and is not sent to any server. There is no sign-in, and we run no server that receives your puzzles, hints, or results.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements: a banner ad at the bottom of some screens, and optional rewarded videos you may choose to watch in exchange for hint coins. The App does not show full-screen ads. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location information
  • App usage data and interaction with ads

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, the App will request your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Consent for Users in the EEA/UK

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present Google's consent form when required, before serving personalized ads, in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You may choose to receive non-personalized ads instead.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect or store any payment information. The App only checks with the App Store whether you own the purchase, so that it can hide banner ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle and is not aimed at children under 13. Since the App asks no one for personal information, it holds none from children either. Ads are chosen by AdMob under Google's own rules for content suitable for children.

Data Retention

Your puzzles, notes, streak, and coins stay on your iPhone or iPad until you delete the App, which removes them all. Nothing from the App is kept on a server of ours.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (including the User Messaging Platform) - for serving advertisements and collecting consent where required. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store - for processing the optional in-app purchase. Apple Privacy Policy

Changes to This Policy

If the App starts handling information differently, for example when a new hint feature or ad format is added, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
