Sudoku: Classic Puzzle & Hints — Support

Sudoku: Classic Puzzle & Hints is a Sudoku app whose hints teach you how to solve, from basic singles up to advanced techniques such as X-Wing, Swordfish and XY-Wing. A hint marks the cells on your board and explains the next logical step, without giving away the number. There are four difficulty levels, a Daily Challenge, and no full-screen ads.

FAQ

  • How do hints work? Tap Hint during a game. The hint marks the cells that give the reason and the cells where candidates can be removed, and explains the step in everyday words with the usual technique name. Longer techniques are split into steps you can page through for free. The number itself is not shown, so you place it yourself.
  • The hint says the board needs a more advanced technique. Hints cover 18 techniques, from singles to X-Wing, Swordfish, XY-Wing, XYZ-Wing, W-Wing, Simple Coloring and X-Chain. A few very hard boards need a step beyond these; the hint then charges nothing. In that case select a cell and use Reveal to have its answer entered for you.
  • How do coins work? You start with 100 coins. A hint costs 30 coins and revealing one cell costs 50. You can earn 50 coins by watching a short video, only when you choose to.
  • Why did a number turn red? It clashes with the same number in its row, column, or box. There is no mistake limit, so fix it whenever you like.
  • Is every puzzle solvable? Yes. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.
  • How does the Daily Challenge work? A new puzzle arrives every day, and every player gets the same board. Solve it each day to build your streak, then share your time and streak as an emoji grid. No answers are included.
  • How do I remove ads? Tap Hint during a game, then Remove Ads (one-time purchase). It hides the banner ads; coin videos stay available when you choose to watch them. On a new device, use Settings → Restore Purchase.
  • Does it need an internet connection? No. Puzzles play offline, and no account is needed. Ads and purchases need a connection.
