Good Neighbors Farm Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Good Neighbors Farm is made by one independent developer. The short version: the game collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. It does not use the network at all.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your stars and best score for each field, and which fields are open.
  • The crop counts in your barn.
  • Whether sound is on or off.

These are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not have

No account or sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifier. The game does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If a later version adds anything that changes this page, such as ads, the page will be updated with a new date before that version is released. Questions: aka84728@gmail.com

となりの畑は、個人の開発者が作っています。このゲームは、使う人の情報を集めず、どこにも送りません。通信も使いません。

iPhoneの中に残すもの

  • 畑ごとの星と一番高い点、開いている畑
  • 納屋に積み上がった作物の数
  • 音を出すかどうか

これらはアプリの中にだけ保存し、開発者にもほかの誰にも送りません。アプリを消すと一緒に消えます。

このゲームに無いもの

アカウントやログイン、広告、アプリ内課金、利用の分析、追跡、広告の識別子はありません。位置情報、連絡先、写真、カメラ、マイクも使いません。

子どもの情報

個人の情報を集めないので、子どもの情報も集めません。
