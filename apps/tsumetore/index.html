Tsume Shogi: Rank Up — Support

Tsume Shogi: Rank Up (昇級詰将棋 - 1手詰から初段へ) is a shogi (Japanese chess) mate-puzzle trainer. 680 puzzles from mate-in-1 to mate-in-3 are arranged in 68 stages of 10. Clear stages to rank up from 15 kyu to Shodan and watch your piece grow from Pawn to King. A puzzle of the day is included as a bonus.

FAQ

  • Where do the puzzles come from? Every puzzle is generated by a shogi engine and then verified by an exhaustive search: exactly one solution, no shorter mate, no leftover attacking pieces, and no illegal pawn-drop mate.
  • How do stages work? Solve all 10 puzzles in a stage to open the next one. Stars show how many you solved without a mistake or a hint. Every 4 stages raises your rank. Your progress in a stage is saved if you leave in the middle.
  • What if I am stuck? After 3 wrong moves, "Show the answer" plays the correct moves. Then you play them yourself to solve the puzzle.
  • What is the puzzle of the day? A bonus puzzle chosen from your device's local date. Solve it to extend your streak. An optional reminder is sent once a day, shortly before the time you usually play; you can turn it off in Settings.
  • How do hints work? On a puzzle you may watch one rewarded video to light up the squares that give check. One hint per puzzle.
  • What are the rules of the defending side? The defender holds every piece not on the board.
  • How do I remove ads? Settings → Remove Ads (one-time purchase). Use "Restore Purchases" on a new device.
