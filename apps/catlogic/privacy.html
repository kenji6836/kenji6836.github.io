Fishy Naps and your privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Fishy Naps (called Nap Spots in earlier versions) is a logic puzzle: every cat needs its own row, column and color, and the fish clues tell you how many cats nap around them. This page follows your information through the game, from the board on your screen to the ads between puzzles.

Your puzzles stay on your device

Fishy Naps has no sign-in and no server of its own, so there is nowhere for us to send your play. Everything it remembers lives on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Which puzzles you have solved, your best times, and the board you left half-finished
  • Your daily puzzle results and your streak
  • Your settings, such as Show timer, Haptics and Auto × around cats

None of this is personal information, and none of it leaves the device. To wipe it, tap Reset progress at the bottom of Settings, or delete the game.

Ads between puzzles (Google AdMob)

The ads in Fishy Naps come from Google AdMob. They appear only after a level puzzle, never while a board is open in front of you. To choose and measure those ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier and similar device identifiers
  • Your IP address
  • Your rough location
  • How you use the game and whether you viewed or tapped an ad

Google describes what it does with this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and its full rules are in the Google Privacy Policy. AdMob is the only outside service built into the game.

Two questions the game may ask you

Tracking. On iOS 14.5 or later, Fishy Naps asks before letting ads follow your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Your answer can be changed whenever you like in the iPhone's Settings app.

Ad consent in the EEA and the UK. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalized, as the GDPR requires. Saying no still lets you play, with non-personalized ads. To change your answer later, open Settings in the game and tap Choose how ads use your data.

Remove Ads

The single purchase in the game, Remove Ads, is bought and restored through Apple's App Store. We never see your card or any other payment details.

Sharing a daily puzzle

When you tap Share after a daily puzzle, the iOS share sheet opens with a short text: the puzzle number, your time and your streak. Nothing else is attached, and it goes only where you send it.

Children

Fishy Naps is a puzzle for everyone and is not aimed at children under 13. We never knowingly gather personal information from a child. Ads are limited to content rated G (general audiences), and AdMob applies its own policies on ads around children's content.
