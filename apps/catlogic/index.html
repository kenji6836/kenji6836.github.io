Nap Spots: Cat Logic Puzzle - Support

Nap Spots is a calm logic puzzle. Place one cat in every row, column and colored area, and never let two cats touch, not even at the corners. Every puzzle has exactly one answer.

FAQ

  • Does every puzzle really have one answer? Yes. Every board is checked by a solver before it ships, so it has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.
  • How do I mark cells? Tap once for ×, twice for a cat, three times to clear. Drag across cells to mark many × at once.
  • I'm stuck. Tap Hint. It first shows any marks that don't match the answer; if there are none, it explains the next step.
  • Can I turn off the timer? Yes. Settings (gear button) > Show timer.
  • How do I start over? Settings > Reset progress erases solved puzzles and best times.
  • How do I remove ads? Settings > Remove ads is a one-time purchase. If you reinstall the App or use a new device, tap Settings > Restore purchase.
