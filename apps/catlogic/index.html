Fishy Naps - Help

Fishy Naps is a calm logic puzzle about sleepy cats and numbered fish. Each board hides one cat for every row, column and colored area. The fish tell you where the cats are napping.

Reading the fish

The number on a fish is how many cats sleep in the 8 cells around it, corners included. A fish with 0 means none of its neighbors has a cat. Cats never sleep on a fish, so you cannot mark a fish cell.

A fish turns red when more cats are around it than its number. Take one of those cats away and the fish goes back to normal.

The other three rules

One cat in every row and column. One cat in every colored area. Two cats never touch, not even at the corners. The four rule cards above the board turn red when a rule is broken and amber when a row, column, area or fish has no room left.

Questions

  • How do I mark cells? Tap once for ×, twice for a cat, three times to clear. Drag across cells to mark many × at once.
  • I'm stuck. Tap Hint. It first shows marks that don't match the answer. If there are none, it explains the next step, including fish steps such as "this fish already has all its cats".
  • Is there only one answer? Yes. Every board is checked by our solver before it ships, so logic alone is enough.
  • Can I hide the timer? Yes. Settings (gear button) > Show timer.
  • How do I start over? Settings > Reset progress erases solved puzzles, best times and your daily streak.
  • How do I remove ads? Settings > Remove ads is a one-time purchase. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap Settings > Restore purchase.
