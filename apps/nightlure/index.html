Night Lure — Support

Night Lure is a turn-based logic puzzle in a paper-model town at night. Zombies walk toward the nearest sound. Drop radios on the streets to lead every zombie into a manhole before one reaches a lit house. There are 30 nights, each with a way to clear it. Nothing is left to luck.

FAQ

  • How do I play? Each turn, tap a street square to drop a radio, or tap WAIT. Then every zombie takes one step toward the closest sound. Get them all into manholes to reach the morning.
  • What if a zombie reaches a house? The night is lost. Tap UNDO to step back a turn, or RETRY to start the night again.
  • What do the fences, lids and traffic lights do? A zombie has to bash a fence twice to break it. A tagged manhole closes after one zombie falls in. Traffic lights go red, red, green, green, and nobody enters on red.
  • The waiting takes long. Can I speed it up? Yes. Turn on fast-forward at the top of the screen. When you are out of radios, WAIT ▸▸ plays the rest of the night for you.
  • Do nights open in order? Yes. Clear a night to open the next one. The NIGHTS list keeps your fewest radios for each night.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? Not in the current version.
