Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Every night you clear in Night Lure stays on the iPhone you play it on. Here is what the app keeps, and what it never receives.

What never reaches me

Your name, email address, whereabouts and device numbers never reach me. All thirty nights are played and checked on your iPhone, and the app never goes online. There is no advertising or analytics code inside, so nothing is passed to any other company either. There is no sign-in.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The nights you have cleared, and your fewest radios on each
  • The night you had open last
  • Whether fast-forward is on or off

These are written only to the app's private storage. To start the town over, delete the app from your iPhone; all three go with it.

About age

Night Lure was made with teenagers and grown-ups in mind who enjoy turn-based logic puzzles. Children under 13 are not its main audience, and it receives nothing from anyone of any age.

Future versions

Before any version that changes what the app receives, such as one with ads, this page will be rewritten first and the date above renewed.
