Blockwise – Block Puzzle — Support

Blockwise is a relaxed 8×8 block puzzle with Foresight, a planning assistant that counts the remaining placements for each piece and warns you before a dead-end move. Works offline, with full VoiceOver support.

FAQ

  • What does Foresight do? It shows how many spots remain for each piece and warns before a move that would leave a piece unplaceable. You can still make the move if you want.
  • How do I remove ads? Settings → Remove Ads (one-time purchase). Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings on a new device.
  • Does the game need an account or internet? No. There is no login, and the game is fully playable offline.
