Blockwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Blockwise is an 8x8 block puzzle you play with Gattino the cat. This page explains what stays on your iPhone, what the ads may use, and how your choices are respected.

What stays on your device

Your board in progress, best score, tuna cans, Foresight and sound settings, and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your device. Blockwise has no account and no server of its own, and it never sends this data anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Blockwise shows ads from Google AdMob between some games and when you choose to watch a short ad for tuna cans. To show and measure ads, Google may use:

  • The advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location
  • How you interact with the ads

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your consent comes first (EEA, UK and similar regions)

Where the law requires consent, Blockwise shows Google's consent form before any ad loads. If you decline, you will not be asked for tracking permission, and only ads that do not use tracking are shown. You can change your choice any time from Settings > Privacy Settings in the app.

Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

If consent is not required, or after you have consented, iOS asks whether Blockwise may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

"Remove Ads" is a single one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Tuna cans cannot be bought; you earn them by playing or by watching an optional ad.

Children

Blockwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
