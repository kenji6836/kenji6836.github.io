Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mission Control Revenue ("the Tool") handles information. The Tool is a personal automation program operated by Kenji Ido ("the developer") and is used only by the developer.

Information the Tool accesses

  • Google AdMob earnings reports of the developer's own AdMob account, obtained through the Google AdMob API using the read-only reporting scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.report).
  • App Store Connect sales reports of the developer's own developer account.

How the information is used

The reports are aggregated into weekly revenue totals and stored on the developer's own computer. The data is used only to track the developer's business performance.

Sharing

No information obtained through Google APIs is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No information is used for advertising or for building user profiles.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the developer's computer. Aggregated totals are stored in local files on the same computer. The developer can revoke the Tool's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The Tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
