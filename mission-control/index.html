Mission Control Revenue

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Mission Control Revenue is a personal automation tool operated by Kenji Ido (the developer of the apps published on this site). It runs on the developer's own computer and records weekly revenue figures into a private dashboard.

What it does

  • Reads the developer's own App Store Connect sales reports.
  • Reads the developer's own Google AdMob earnings reports through the AdMob API (read-only report access).
  • Writes the weekly totals into a local spreadsheet that only the developer can see.

Who can use it

The tool is not distributed and has no users other than its developer. It is registered with Google solely so that the developer can authorize access to their own AdMob account.
